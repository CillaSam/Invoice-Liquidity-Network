# On-call Escalation and Handoff

Issue #1329 adds a fail-closed operational control for mainnet readiness. The
source of truth is [`ops/on-call-checklist.json`](https://github.com/Invoice-Liquidity-Network/Invoice-Liquidity-Network/blob/dev/ops/on-call-checklist.json).

## Control owner and recovery

- **Control owner:** @infrastructure-lead
- **On-call first responder:** @sre-oncall
- **Secondary/recovery owner:** @devops-oncall
- **Escalation owner:** @security-lead

If the check fails, do not bypass it. Follow the recovery table in the
[operator runbook](https://github.com/Invoice-Liquidity-Network/Invoice-Liquidity-Network/blob/dev/docs/runbooks/on-call-escalation-handoff.md),
then rerun the gate.

## What is enforced

- Every ownership role is named and free of placeholders.
- Acknowledgement (15 min) and P1 escalation (15 min) targets are enforced.
- The next handoff date must equal the last handoff plus the rotation interval.
- A handoff past its due date fails the build; a handoff inside the warning
  window produces a non-failing warning.
- Every checklist item has a unique id, an owner, and a live runbook anchor.
- This page, the legacy docs nav entry, and the mainnet checklist artifact link
  must all exist.
